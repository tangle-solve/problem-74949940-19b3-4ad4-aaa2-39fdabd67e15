### BCGT-000226: Many-miner private-fork equilibria
- **Subfield:** Selfish mining, withholding strategies and attack rationality
- **Open:** Characterize equilibrium withholding and release policies for arbitrarily many heterogeneous miners maintaining private forks under incomplete information.
- **Status:** Research target extending 2016 mining games; 2026 multi-attacker experiments compare specified strategies without solving general equilibria.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Competing attackers can invalidate single-attacker predictions.
- **Key works:** Kiayias et al. (2016), *Blockchain Mining Games*, arXiv:1607.02420; Perešíni et al. (2026), *Selfish Mining in Multi-Attacker Scenarios: An Empirical Evaluation of Nakamoto, Fruitchain, and Strongchain*, arXiv:2601.02984.
